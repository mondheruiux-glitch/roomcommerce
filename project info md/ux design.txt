Accessibility Specification (WCAG 2.1 Level AA)Visual & Non-Visual ParitySkip Link Implementation: Every render screen contains an invisible skip-nav link focused on first tab: <a href="#accessible-sku-table" class="sr-only focus:not-sr-only">Skip to accessible product list</a>.Contrast Ratios: All body text meets $\ge 4.5:1$ contrast against backgrounds; large titles and action buttons meet $\ge 3.0:1$.Touch Targets: All interactive pins and buttons maintain a minimum tap target of $48 \times 48\text{px}$ with at least $8\text{px}$ spacing between adjacent targets to prevent mis-taps.Keyboard Navigation & Focus TrappingCanvas Pins: Each spatial pin is a focusable <button aria-label="Coffee Table: Solid Oak Round Table, $249.99 at Target">. Tabbing navigates sequentially through pins in reading order (top-left to bottom-right).Modal Sheets (SCR-08A, SCR-09): Focus is trapped inside open modal sheets. Pressing Escape closes the sheet and returns focus directly to the originating pin element.Announcements: Status changes on SCR-07 utilize aria-live="polite" to announce phase transitions without interrupting assistive speech.5.
