MVP Definition & Scoping PrinciplesThe Core MVP ObjectiveThe smallest complete product must deliver on a single critical user journey: A user takes a photo of an empty or cluttered room, selects a style and budget ceiling, and receives a realistic redesigned room where every newly placed item is an interactive, in-stock, purchasable retail product with an active affiliate link.Scoping GuardrailsThe "No-Fake-Product" Rule: The AI pipeline must never output purely synthetic decor without matching it to a real-world SKU.Platform Focus: Web-first responsive application (mobile and desktop browsers) with direct Stripe checkout. Native iOS/StoreKit is postponed to P1 to avoid app review delays and separate codebases during initial learning.Non-Negotiable Guardrails: Security, user privacy, FTC commercial compliance, WCAG accessibility, and basic error-handling states are foundational to P0 and cannot be compromised.┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

4. Riskiest Assumptions Requiring Validation in MVPThe MVP is engineered specifically to extract empirical answers to four existential assumptions:ASSUMPTION-01 (Visual Similarity Acceptance): Users will accept a real catalog product that is an 80–90% visual match to the AI render rather than abandoning because it is not an exact duplicate.MVP Test: Measure product detail view time and outbound click rate across confidence brackets ($\ge 90\%$ match vs. $80–89\%$ match).ASSUMPTION-02 (Downstream Commercial Intent): Users seeing an AI-redesigned room with tagged products will click external retailer links with genuine purchase intent rather than treating the app as passive entertainment.MVP Test: Track the Outbound Affiliate CTR (Target: $\ge 25\%$) and repeat session returns within 14 days.ASSUMPTION-03 (Episodic Pass Monetization): High-intent movers will pay a one-time fee of $14.99 for a 30-day Move-In Pass to furnish an apartment without churn-inducing recurring subscriptions.MVP Test: Track the Free-to-Pass conversion rate at the paywall gate (Target: $\ge 7.0\%$).ASSUMPTION-04 (GPU Unit Economic Viability): Monocular depth and inpainting pipelines can be executed for $\le \$0.04$ per generation on serverless GPU hardware without exceeding the 15-second latency budget.MVP Test: Measure end-to-end p90 generation latency and total inference compute invoice per completed session.5. MVP Release Criteria (Go / No-Go Gate)The MVP will not be released to public acquisition channels until it meets the following objective, testable thresholds:Functional & Algorithmic Quality[ ] End-to-End Pipeline Success: $\ge 95\%$ of valid room uploads successfully yield a rendered room with interactive pins on the first attempt without server exception errors.[ ] Catalog Alignment: 100% of generated product pins display items that are verified as currently in-stock in the indexed catalog database.[ ] Generation Latency: End-to-end rendering and SKU retrieval completes in $\le 15.0\text{ seconds}$ (p90) across test network connections.Safety, Legal & Privacy[ ] Automated Face Obfuscation: 100% of detectable human faces in test benchmark photos receive Gaussian blur prior to server-side inpainting.[ ] Metadata Erasure: 100% of uploaded images have EXIF/GPS telemetry stripped before persisting to cloud object storage.[ ] Ephemeral Deletion: Automated lifecycle rules verify that raw room images are hard-deleted after 7 days.[ ] Legal Disclosures: Dynamic FTC affiliate disclaimers are visibly rendered on all screens displaying shoppable product links.Commercial & Payments[ ] Affiliate Tag Persistence: Outbound clicks correctly append active affiliate tracking parameters and redirect to valid merchant URLs without 404/410 errors.[ ] Payment Processing: Stripe Checkout successfully provisions the 30-day Move-In Pass entitlement upon receipt of the checkout.session.completed webhook.Accessibility[ ] Screen Reader Parity: The structured DOM alternative shopping list exposes all rendered product titles, prices, dimensions, and outbound links conforming to WCAG 2.1 AA.
