Engineering implementation is blocked until Phase 1 (Product & UX Architecture) and Phase 2 (Technical & Systems Architecture) receive full sign-off.Active Critical Risks: Visual search discrepancy (hallucinated items vs. real SKUs); catalog inventory staleness; regional geo-routing failure; GPU inference unit costs outstripping affiliate referral margins.Immediate Next Step: Address essential discovery clarifications to unlock Phase 1 Product Requirements Documentation (PRD).Project Foundation & Discovery Overview1. Problem StatementValidated Evidence: Existing generative AI interior design tools produce aesthetically appealing room transformations but populate scenes with synthetic, non-existent furniture. Users who want to purchase what they see face high friction trying to manually reverse-image search items.Identified Pain Point: The disconnect between spatial inspiration and commercial execution leads to drop-off; consumers lack an integrated pipeline that maps spatial redesigns directly to purchasable, in-stock inventory matching their real room dimensions.2. Target User PersonasPrimary Persona (The Aesthetic Upgrader): Renters or new homeowners looking to furnish an entire room or refresh decor within a defined aesthetic (e.g., Japandi, Industrial) and budget tier, seeking instant shopping lists without hiring an interior designer.Secondary Persona (The Spatial Optimizer): Homeowners tackling specific functional spaces (e.g., home offices, patios, living areas) who want to preserve core existing pieces while replacing auxiliary decor and furniture.3. Business Opportunity & Monetization ThesisAffiliate Referral Capture: High-intent visual commerce yields higher conversion than cold catalog browsing. Capturing 3% to 8% affiliate commission on medium-to-high ticket items ($200–$2,000 basket sizes).Dual-Monetization Engine [ASSUMPTION]: Combining an in-app subscription/freemium model (covering compute and high-resolution rendering costs) with affiliate revenue (driving scalable gross margin).4. Baseline Assumption Matrix[ASSUMPTION - CRITICAL]: Either computer vision/reverse visual search or catalog-constrained inpainting can deliver a product match accuracy rate exceeding 80% perceived similarity to the rendered design.[ASSUMPTION - HIGH]: Aggregator affiliate networks (e.g., Skimlinks, Impact, Amazon Associates) provide sufficient API uptime, programmatic deep-linking, and geo-localization to support a seamless multi-country user experience.[ASSUMPTION - MEDIUM]: Users are willing to upload clear interior photos of their personal living environments without elevated privacy friction, provided data handling is transparent.Essential Clarification QuestionsBefore finalizing Phase 0 sign-off and entering Phase 1 (Product Requirements), the council requires decisions
